# Privacy Policy for Collins Community AME

Effective September 18, 2026

Collins Community AME is an app for Scripture reading, church information, and personal worship activities. This policy describes the data handling in the current iPhone and iPad app and its Home Screen widget.

## Personal information in My Faith

The Prayer Journal, Personal Notes, and Weekly Worship Checklist are private on-device features. Their content and completion status are stored in the app's local storage on the device. The church does not receive, review, or synchronize this content through the app. No account is required.

Users can edit or delete individual prayers and notes in the app and reset the checklist. The checklist also resets when a new calendar week begins. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data. Depending on device backup settings, a copy may remain in an iCloud or computer backup controlled by the user.

The app does not currently offer cross-device syncing or an in-app export for this data.

## Bible requests

The app includes a small number of Bible passages locally. When a user searches for another passage, the app sends the requested Bible reference to `bible-api.com` over HTTPS to retrieve King James Version text. The service may receive ordinary network information associated with that request, such as the user's IP address, the requested reference, and request time. The church does not control that service's data practices. Users can avoid this request by using the bundled passages or not performing an online lookup.

Prayer journal entries, notes, and checklist activity are not included in Bible lookup requests.

## Home screen and widget verses

The Home screen selects a motivational verse from a list bundled with the app when it launches and remembers the previously shown verse locally to avoid an immediate repeat. The Home Screen widget selects a verse from its own bundled list each day. Neither feature needs a network request or access to the journal, notes, or checklist.

## Other data practices

The current app does not require registration and does not include advertising, an in-app analytics service, or a church announcement service. It does not request access to contacts, photos, the microphone, camera, or precise location. The church address displayed in the app is static information, not the user's location.

The app itself does not transmit My Faith content to the church. Apple may separately process App Store downloads, device backups, and diagnostic information under the user's Apple settings and Apple's own privacy terms.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Personal journal and note content remains on the device as described above.

For questions about this policy, contact Collins Community AME Church through its official channels or by mail at 4125 N.E. 16th Street, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma 73117.

We may revise this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when that happens.
